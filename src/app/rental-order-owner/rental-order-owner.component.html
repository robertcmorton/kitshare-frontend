Pricing and Payments:

We follow industry standards set by rental houses on how we charge for shoot days. There is no charge for the pick up and return days. If you rent equipment for 1, 2 or 3 shoot days, you pay for the shoot days only. If you rent equipment between 4 and 7 days, you only pay for 3 shoot days. Weekends are considered 1 shoot day. The cost of your rental is based on the number of shoots days. Additional details regarding pricing and payments are available at https://www.kitshare.com.au/faq/list/Pricing-and-Payments
